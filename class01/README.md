## What's AI?

A quest to imitate the human brain, make a machine brain that canmimic the kinds of tasks that we think are unique to humans.

1. Naturalist intelligence: ability to discriminate among living things
2. Musical intelligence: enables us to recognize, create, reproduce, and reflect on music
3. Logical mathematical (computational) intelligence: ability to calculate, quantify, consider proportions
4. existential intelligence
5. interpersonal intelligence
6. bodily-kinesthetic intelligence
7. linguistic intelligence: the ability to think in words to use language to express 
8. intrapersonal intelligence
